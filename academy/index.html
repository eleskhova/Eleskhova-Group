Most learners reach matric having never heard of a real career in supply chain or logistics. Eleskhova Academy runs half-day sessions at partner high schools across Gauteng showing what the industry actually looks like day-to-day — then hands learners straight into TETA's funded learnership programme, with Eleskhova Academy as their training provider from day one. Built for Grade 11–12 learners and matriculants, especially those who didn't go on to university or college.
